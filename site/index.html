How it works

Peer-to-peer, end to end

A Frith workspace is a multi-writer log replicated over an encrypted peer-to-peer network. Your messages travel from your machine to your teammates' machines — and nowhere else.

🛰

Invites, not accounts

Paste an invite and your machine becomes a writer in the space. There's no account with us — because there is no "us" in the middle.

🔐

Private means physically private

Private channels aren't a permissions flag on someone's server. They're separate encrypted logs with their own keys — peers outside them never even hold the bytes.

📴

Works offline, syncs when you meet

Write on a plane, sync at the office. Any two members of a space can sync with each other directly — no coordinator required.

Your data, your policies

You decide what you store — and what you share

In a cloud app, retention is a pricing tier. In Frith it's a policy you set, per space and per device. Sensible defaults, full control.

Recent by defaultJoin a busy space and you sync recent history only. The ten-year archive doesn't land on your laptop unless you ask for it.
Backfill on requestNeed the deep history? Request it from peers who hold it — as far back as you want, up to the limits you set.
Size capsLarge files are capped and fetched lazily. A 4 GB screen recording never auto-downloads to your phone.
Sharing layersChoose what leaves your device: what you share with a space, what you keep local, and what you'll relay for others.
Verified contentFiles are content-addressed and hash-verified, from senders you chose to admit. Tampered or spoofed data doesn't replicate.
Institutional memory

Ask your team's history — without shipping it to a datacenter

The knowledge that today lives in people's heads and dies in scrollback becomes something you can ask. All of it runs on local models, on your hardware.

💬

Answers with receipts

Ask "why did we pick this vendor?" and get a cited answer pointing at the actual threads, calls, and people — not a hallucinated summary.

🎙

Calls become knowledge

Campfire calls are transcribed and summarized on-device. Decisions and action items flow into the same searchable memory.

🖥

Local AI, literally

Generation, embeddings, and transcription run on your machines via local models. Your company's memory never leaves the company.

Communities

Start a fire, gather your people

Frith isn't only for companies. A space costs nothing to create and nothing to run — so make one for your open-source project, your study group, your neighborhood, your guild.

🔥

Free forever, structurally

There's no server bill to pass on to you. A community of 5 or 5,000 runs on its members' own machines.

🧭

An opt-in directory

List your space publicly so people can find it, or stay invite-only and invisible. Discovery is a choice, never a default.

🛡

Moderation with teeth

Admits are explicit, writers are revocable, and blocked peers stop replicating to you — not just hidden behind a mute.

Roadmap

Where this is going

Built in the open. The chat core works today; here's what's next.

Now

P2P chat core

Spaces, channels, DMs, threads, calls — replicating peer-to-peer with no central database.

Next

Desktop app

A proper desktop client that manages your local AI models, storage policies, and notifications — your node, running quietly in the tray.

Next

Local files & repos

Point Frith at your codebase and docs. Ask questions that connect a decision in chat to the commit that implemented it and the doc that explains it.

Later

Knowledge graph search

People, threads, files, commits, and decisions as one connected graph — so "who knows about X?" is a query, not a guess.
